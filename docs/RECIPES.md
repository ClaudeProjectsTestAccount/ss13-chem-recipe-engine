### Cleaner smoke (45 tiles)

1. Dispenser: 15u Phosphorus  (1 click at 15u)  
   `beaker: 15u Phosphorus | syringe: empty`
2. Syringe: draw 15u from the beaker (got 15.00u)  
   `beaker: empty | syringe: 15u Phosphorus`
3. Beaker: spill/empty the rest  
   `beaker: empty | syringe: 15u Phosphorus`
4. Dispenser: 15u Hydrogen  (1 click at 15u)  
   `beaker: 15u Hydrogen | syringe: 15u Phosphorus`
5. Dispenser: 5u Nitrogen  (1 click at 5u)  
   `beaker: 15u Ammonia | syringe: 15u Phosphorus`
6. Dispenser: 15u Water  (1 click at 15u)  
   `beaker: 30u Space Cleaner | syringe: 15u Phosphorus`
7. Dispenser: 15u Potassium  (1 click at 15u)  
   `beaker: 30u Space Cleaner, 15u Potassium | syringe: 15u Phosphorus`
8. Dispenser: 15u Sugar  (1 click at 15u)  
   `beaker: 30u Space Cleaner, 15u Potassium, 15u Sugar | syringe: 15u Phosphorus`
9. Syringe: set transfer 15u, inject all 15u into the beaker  **<-- TRIGGER**  
   `beaker: empty | syringe: empty`

## New reactions

A second batch, picked from the reaction list for effects people do not usually know about, several of which need bigger beakers or syringes. ### Metal foam - instant walls (100 tiles)

**Risk:** use with care.  **Needs:** a chem heater.

**What it does:** Aluminium foam. About 8 seconds after it appears, every foam tile leaves a foamed-metal wall: dense, airtight, 20 HP, and on a space tile it also lays plating first. Seals hull breaches and corridors instantly.

1. Dispenser: 5u Sulfuric Acid  (1 click at 5u)  
   `beaker: 5u Sulfuric Acid | syringe: empty`
2. Dispenser: 5u Fluorine  (1 click at 5u)  
   `beaker: 5u Sulfuric Acid, 5u Fluorine | syringe: empty`
3. Dispenser: 25u Hydrogen  (1 click at 25u)  
   `beaker: 5u Sulfuric Acid, 5u Fluorine, 25u Hydrogen | syringe: empty`
4. Dispenser: 5u Potassium  (1 click at 5u)  
   `beaker: 5u Sulfuric Acid, 5u Fluorine, 25u Hydrogen, 5u Potassium | syringe: empty`
5. Heater: set target 400K, put the beaker in - the reaction fires after ~32s (beaker ~381K); you can take it out then [heater speed 0.05]  
   `beaker: 20u Hydrogen, 20u Fluorosulfuric Acid | syringe: empty`
6. Dispenser: 20u Lithium  (1 click at 20u)  
   `beaker: 20u Fluorosulfuric Acid, 20u Foaming Agent | syringe: empty`
7. Dispenser: 100u Aluminium  (1 click at 100u)  
   `beaker: empty | syringe: empty`

**Result:** Foam over up to 100 tiles, each tile holds nothing (the foam only leaves its structure).

**Notes:** Needs the chem heater for the fluorosulfuric acid step. The last click is the 100u aluminium button: the dispenser stops at the free space (60u), which is exactly the 3:1 aluminium the 20u of foaming agent/acid can use. No acid is left over.

**Caution:** Walls form on every foamed tile, including ones people stand on, so you can box people in. Never foam over injured crew or block medbay/escape routes. Bare hands bounce off the walls; any tool or weapon breaks them.

**Order:** ORDER MATTERS - 24 of 48 dispense orders work; follow the steps exactly.

Engine warnings: beaker full: dispenser click of 100u was clamped to 60u (expected where the recipe relies on the dispenser stopping at the free space).

Source: `recipes/others.dm (metalfoam), effects_foam.dm (foamedmetal, 20 integrity, foam_floor)`
